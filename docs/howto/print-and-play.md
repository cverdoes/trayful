# Print and play

Trayful can print every component at its real size, to cut out and play at a real table.

1. Open your project on a table and pick **Print and play** from the **More** menu in the toolbar. A page opens with your components laid out on A4 sheets.
2. Use your browser's print dialog (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P). Choose **Save as PDF** for a file, or a printer, and set the scale to **100%** (sometimes called "actual size"), not "fit to page".
3. Cut along the edges.

Flippable components, such as cards and two-sided tokens and tiles, are followed by a sheet of their backs, mirrored, so the backs line up when you print double-sided and flip on the long edge. Anything larger than the printable area of a sheet, such as a big board, is shrunk to fit a sheet of its own; print it larger at a copy shop if you need it full size.

Sizes are millimetres throughout, so a 63 × 88 mm card prints at 63 × 88 mm. Measure one card after printing to check that nothing was scaled.

The page shows what is in the box when you open it, including changes you saved since opening the table.
